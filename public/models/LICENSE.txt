Riders - models supplied by the project owner
---------------------------------------------
  runer.glb  panda.glb

  These were provided as high-detail static GLBs (~83 MB / ~29 MB, no skeleton).
  They were then optimised and auto-rigged for this project with Blender using
  the scripts in tools/ (see tools/optimize_model.py and tools/rig_model.py):

    1. Decimated to ~45k / ~30k triangles.
    2. Embedded textures resized to 1024 and re-encoded as WebP.
    3. Given a simple five-bone spine and a looping "Idle" clip.
